Capabilities

Eight lines of board training and pre-emptive cyber risk work.

  1. Training

    Cyber Enterprise Risk Management Training

    For board members and C-suite executives.

    You receive cyber risk training for your board or executive team, on site or by webinar.

    Designed for Board members or C-Suite executives (on site or via Webinar). By NACD® credentialed experts.

  2. Board

    Board View Cyber Risk Assessment

    For boards and their committees.

    You receive a third-party report on material cyber threats you did not know about.

    A third-party report identifying material cyber threats previously unknown to your organization based on NACD ® and World Economic Forum ® and Global D&O ISAO © Cyber Risk Recommendations for Boards.

  3. Supply

    Supply Chain Risk Assessment

    For general counsel, security leaders and procurement.

    You receive a report on which suppliers present a cyber risk to your organization.

    A report identifying which suppliers are infecting your organization and if they present a cyber risk.

  4. M&A

    M&A Risk Assessment

    For deal teams, general counsel and private equity.

    You receive a report on whether a target company is already compromised or breached.

    a report detailing whether a target company is compromised or breached (e.g. identifying if i.p. has been stolen).

  5. External

    External Security Posture Assessment

    For boards, general counsel and security leaders.

    You receive a report on what outsiders can see, including a dark-web and deep-net search.

    A report detailing what bad actors can see from the "outside-in" (includes dark-web, deep-net search for compromises).

  6. Program

    Cybersecurity Program Assessment

    For executive teams and security leaders.

    You receive a third-party report on gaps between your program and current threats.

    An expedient third-party report identifying gaps between current programs verses current threats.

  7. Internal

    Internal Security Posture Assessment

    For security leaders and executive teams.

    You receive a secure third-party log review for espionage and unsignatured threats.

    A third-party, secure, and expedient review of logs identifying espionage and other unsignatured threats.

  8. Defense

    Cyber Proactive Defense®

    For general counsel and security leaders who need to act on a finding.

    You receive discrete services, within U.S. and/or international law, that stop the attack.

    Discrete services performed within the U.S. and/or international law that stop the attack or eliminate liability. We then offer a strategy for Axon to neutralize these threats within U.S. and/or international law.
