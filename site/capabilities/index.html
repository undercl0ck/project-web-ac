Board View Cyber Risk Assessment

A third-party report of material cyber threats the organization had not already identified, written for the board.

M&A Risk Assessment

A report on whether a company being acquired is compromised, including whether intellectual property has been taken.

Supply Chain Risk Assessment

A report identifying suppliers that present a cyber risk to the organization.

Cybersecurity Program Assessment

A third-party report of gaps between the current program and current threats.

External Security Posture Assessment (dark-web and deep-net search)

A report of what is visible from outside the organization, including a dark-web and deep-net search for compromises.

Internal Security Posture Assessment

A third-party review of logs for espionage and other threats that do not match a known signature.

Discrete services performed within U.S. or international law, directed at stopping an attack.
