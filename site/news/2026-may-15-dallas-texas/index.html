Axon Global and ONI Group Announce Strategic Alliance to Integrate Advanced Cybersecurity, AI, and Enterprise Risk Management into Next-Generation Unmanned Aerial Systems

Partnership Also Incorporates Captis Intelligence Platform to Deliver Comprehensive Cyber-Protected UAS Solutions for Defense and Critical Operations

Axon Global (Axon), a leader in proactive cyber defense and AI-enabled enterprise risk management, today announced a strategic alliance with ONI Group, a premier designer and manufacturer of advanced unmanned aerial systems (UAS) for defense, law enforcement, and specialized operations.

As a designated value-added reseller and broker for ONI Group’s comprehensive UAS catalogue — which includes multi-role quadcopters, heavy-lift hexacopters, fire-fighting platforms, and strategic long-range assets such as VTOL fixed-wing and delta-wing kamikaze systems — Axon Global will integrate its proprietary proactive cyber defense, AI-powered boardroom intelligence, and cyber enterprise risk management (ERM) frameworks directly into ONI’s unmanned systems ecosystem.

This alliance enhances the resilience and operational security of ONI’s platforms, which feature capabilities such as ISR (Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance), precision strikes, fiber-optic EW-protected guidance, heavy payload delivery, and high-endurance operations. By embedding Axon’s solutions, ONI Group UAS will benefit from pre-emptive threat neutralization, hardened command-and-control protections, supply chain risk governance, and real-time cyber situational awareness — critical in contested electronic warfare environments.

The partnership further incorporates Captis Intelligence, whose I-4 platform aggregates intelligence from over 100 million public criminal records, the dark web, and deep web. This integration adds powerful pre- and post-incident intelligence capabilities, enabling enhanced threat actor identification, subject intelligence, and operational risk assessment for UAS missions.

Israel Martinez, CEO of Axon Global, commented: “ONI Group’s advanced unmanned systems represent the cutting edge of tactical and strategic aerial capabilities. By layering Axon’s proactive cyber defense, AI-driven risk governance, and our strategic partnership with Captis Intelligence, we are delivering truly cyber-hardened platforms that operators can trust in high-threat environments. This alliance ensures our warfighters and first responders maintain dominance across the cyber and physical domains.”

The collaboration positions both organizations to support U.S. and allied defense requirements with secure, intelligent, and mission-ready unmanned systems — ranging from rapid-deploy quadcopters and heavy-lift hexacopters to long-range strategic assets with ranges up to 2,000 km.
