Axon Global CEO, published article is featured in Forbes. Axon CEO Israel Martinez, is an internationally recognized expert in Cyber Enterprise Risk management, also recognized by the U.S. Secret Service Office of Strategic Intelligence, the Department of Homeland Security Cybersecurity Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), the Wall Street Journal MarketWatch, and the National Association of Corporate Directors (NACD) [see published recognitions]. On March 11th, Martinez published an article regarding the Cybersecurity Act of 2015 that was featured in Forbes. The article focused on the benefits of the Cybersecurity Act of 2015 and how as a Cyber ERM risk management tool, it is one of the best, if not the best Cyber ERM tool available to the private sector.

Axon Global is a member of the Forbes Technology Council; a founding member of the American University Cyber Governance Center; and an industry advisor to Cyber Storm for DHS CISA. Israel Martinez, CEO for Axon, is a Board Leadership Fellow of NACD, and a board member for the American Red Cross – NTX. He’s been an advisor as a bi-partisan cyber expert across multiple presidential administrations including joint cyber risk briefings with the White House Office of Science, Technology and Policy (OSTP) under President Obama and as an advisor to the Presidential Transition Team under President Trump. Martinez was also simultaneously nominated as a “Top 100 Director” to NACD by all of the following, the U.S. Secret Service Office of Strategic Intelligence headquarters in Washington D.C.; the former CFO for the U.S. Treasury; the Dean for American University; the former President of the Hispanic National Bar Association; the former Managing Executive for Policy & Staff for the U.S. Securities & Exchange Commission (SEC); the former President for the Association for Corporate Growth (ACG), one of the largest private equity organizations in the U.S.; the CTO of one of the most cyber secure banks in the U.S. as published by the Wall Street Journal; and a 14-year award winning veteran of the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA).

Axon Global is a preemptive risk management company that leverages AI and cyber counterintelligence to intercept threats before a breach. It fulfills all the requirements of an ethnic minority owned company. Axon customers and key executives include c-suite and board members from Fortune 500 and Global 1K; a former Presidential Appointee to the U.S. Secret Service; a former CISO of the year by CSO magazine; a former Presidential Appointee to the State Department under two presidential administrations; the former co-author of the DHS program known as Information and Sharing Analysis Centers (ISACs and ISAOs); a former senior oversight executive for the Defense Intelligence Agency; the former Chief Software Officer for the U.S. Airforce at the pentagon; the previous President of the Hispanic National Bar Association; a former DHS Sr. Intelligence Officer and former director for one of the largest Fusion Centers in the U.S.. It also includes over ten members that have held or currently hold top secret clearances. Axon is a vetted contractor to DHS CISA authorized to operate under special privileges as stipulated by the Cybersecurity Act of 2015, Division N.
