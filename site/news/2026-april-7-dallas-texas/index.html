Axon Global CEO Israel Martinez Delivers Key Innovation Message at HMG Strategy’s 18th Annual Dallas C-Level Technology Leadership Summit VIP Dinner Kickoff.

The Exclusive VIP Dinner and Reception Features Insightful Discussion on AI, Cybersecurity, and Leading Like a CEO

Israel Martinez, CEO of Axon Global (Axon Cyber), was invited as the featured speaker at the exclusive dinner and reception for VIP CIOs and CISOs during HMG Strategy’s 18th Annual Dallas C-Level Technology Leadership Summit, held on April 8, 2026, at the prestigious Stonebriar Country Club in Frisco, Texas.

The invitation-only dinner kicked off the summit’s programming and brought together top-tier technology executives for high-level networking and strategic dialogue. Hosted by HMG Strategy — the world’s leading “no-analyst” advisory service for CIOs, CISOs, and other C-level technology leaders — the event focused on the most pressing issues facing enterprise technology leadership today, including AI and data strategy, cybersecurity excellence, and iconic leadership.

Martinez addressed the elite audience on proactive cyber defense, AI-enabled boardroom intelligence, and the evolving role of technology executives in navigating today’s complex risk environment. His remarks emphasized practical strategies for organizations to reclaim cyber dominance while building resilient leadership in an era of rapid technological change and heightened stakeholder expectations.

The summit featured sessions on “Leading Like a CEO,” exploring how technology leaders can drive enterprise transformation with resilience, purpose, and visionary decision-making. Additional programming highlighted cutting-edge approaches to AI implementation, data strategy, and cybersecurity governance. The evening also included recognition through the Global Leadership Institute Awards.

“Speaking to this distinguished group of CIOs and CISOs at HMG Strategy’s Dallas Summit was both an honor and a valuable opportunity to share real-world insights on building proactive cyber strategies that protect enterprise value and reputation,” said Israel Martinez, CEO of Axon Global. “In today’s environment, technology leaders must think and lead like CEOs — balancing innovation, risk management, and long-term resilience.”

HMG Strategy’s C-Level Technology Leadership Summits are renowned for their peer-to-peer format, bringing together C-suite executives from major enterprises to share best practices on business and technology challenges. The Dallas event continued this tradition by fostering meaningful connections among leaders responsible for driving AI adoption, strengthening cybersecurity postures, and shaping the future of their organizations.

At last year’s event, on April 22nd 2025, Israel Martinez, took center stage at HMG Strategy’s 14th Annual Washington D.C. C-Level Technology Leadership Summit to deliver a dynamic presentation and lead a high-profile executive discussion at a gathering of public and private sector leaders focused on the future of technology and cybersecurity.

Following his keynote, Martinez moderated a powerful Executive Fireside Chat with Bridget Bean, the Senior Official Performing the Duties of Director of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA). The discussion, titled “Building Cyber Resilience Through Partnerships: 2025 and Beyond,” explored the pivotal role of public-private collaboration in combating escalating cyber threats.
