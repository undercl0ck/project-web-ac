Axon Global announces a reseller agreement and partnership with Captis Intelligence to Deliver Advanced Facial Recognition solutions integrated AI-Powered Cyber Threat Intelligence and Risk Management Solutions.

Collaboration Combines Proactive Cyber Defense with Deep Dark Web, Deep Web, and Criminal Intelligence Capabilities

Axon Global (Axon Cyber), a leader in AI-enabled cyber enterprise risk management, proactive cyber defense, and cyber counterintelligence solutions, today announced a strategic partnership with Captis Intelligence, a pioneering provider of cloud-based subject intelligence systems.

Effective March 5, 2026, the partnership integrates Axon Global’s advanced cyber risk governance, pre-emptive threat neutralization, and boardroom intelligence capabilities with Captis Intelligence’s powerful I-4 platform (Intelligence, Information, Investigation, Identification). Captis draws from over 100 million public criminal files, the dark web, and the deep web to deliver actionable intelligence for security and asset protection.

Together, the two companies will offer organizations an integrated solution that combines real-time cyber defense with comprehensive pre- and post-incident intelligence. This powerful synergy enables clients to identify hidden risks, detect cyber espionage and insider threats earlier, strengthen supply chain security, and significantly improve overall risk posture.

Israel Martinez, CEO of Axon Global, stated: “We are excited to partner with Captis Intelligence. By combining our proactive cyber defense and governance expertise with Captis’ extensive intelligence database and investigative capabilities, we can deliver a truly comprehensive solution that helps organizations reclaim cyber dominance and protect what matters most — people, assets, and operations.”

The strategic alliance will initially focus on serving Global 1,000 companies, government contractors, and organizations with high national security or critical infrastructure requirements. Future phases will explore expanded international opportunities aligned with U.S. allies.

Captis Intelligence’s technology has been developed since 2014 to create robust security ecosystems that help clients deter and identify criminal activity through open-source intelligence and advanced digital scanning. When paired with Axon Global’s proactive defense framework and AI-driven risk management strategies, clients gain a force-multiplier advantage against evolving threats including supply chain compromises, credential hijacking, and state-sponsored cyber espionage.

This partnership reflects a shared commitment to innovation, proactive defense, and delivering intelligence-driven outcomes that go beyond traditional cybersecurity tools.

About Axon Global Axon Global (Axon Cyber) provides AI-enabled proactive cyber defense, enterprise risk management, and counterintelligence solutions. The company helps boards, C-suites, and senior leadership reclaim cyber dominance through practical governance, rapid risk identification, and pre-emptive strategies without excessive operational friction. Visit: www.axoncyber.com

About Captis Intelligence Captis Intelligence delivers cloud-based subject intelligence through its I-4 platform, aggregating data from public criminal records, the dark web, and deep web. Founded in 2014, Captis helps organizations and individuals enhance security and asset protection by turning intelligence into actionable insights for investigation, identification, and threat mitigation.
